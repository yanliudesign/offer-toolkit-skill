# Recruiter Playbook

**20 种招聘官典型话术 + 每种的应对**。生成 [`../prompts/counter-simulation.md`](../prompts/counter-simulation.md) 时，从这里选 3 种最相关的做 deep simulation；其他的塞进 HTML §5 附录"更多招聘官话术速查表"。

**核心原则**：招聘官不是敌人。他是**你未来 3-5 年的 comp advocate**。目标是让他能"回去 sell 你的 ask"，而不是让他觉得受挫。

---

## Category A · Anchor / 起手式（谈判开始时）

### A1 · "What are you looking for in comp?"（recruiter 首次接触时的经典陷阱）
- **陷阱**：你先报数字 = 你自己 anchor 自己，通常 anchor 太低
- **应对**：`"I'd love to first understand the range for this level at [Company]. Once I have a sense of your bands, I can share where I'm targeting."` — 让他先报
- **如果他坚持**：`"I'm focused on competitive total comp for the level. What's the range you have in mind for this role?"`

### A2 · "What's your current comp?"（很多州现在违法问，但仍会被问）
- **应对（合法州）**：`"I'd prefer to focus on the value I'd bring to this role, and what's competitive for the level, rather than what I make today. Happy to discuss target comp for this position."`
- **应对（违法问的州，CA / NY / WA / MA…)**：`"I'll respect [state] pay transparency law and focus on this role's compensation range."`
- **如果你已经透露过**：`"Since our initial conversation, I've been thinking about total comp more holistically for this move. What matters most is the fit and the full package."` — soft reset

### A3 · "Our budget for this role is $X"（recruiter 先 anchor 你）
- **陷阱**：接受 $X 作为 ceiling
- **应对**：`"Thanks for sharing. Can you help me understand how that breaks down — base, equity, sign-on? And is $X the top of the range or the midpoint?"` —— 拆解 anchor

## Category B · Best Offer / 天花板 (回应你 counter 时)

### B1 · "This is our best offer"
- 见 [`../prompts/counter-simulation.md`](../prompts/counter-simulation.md) Pushback #1

### B2 · "We built this offer with your background in mind"
- **意思**：软性 anchor — "别 push，这已经 personalized 过了"
- **应对**：`"I appreciate that, and it's clear you've thought about it. Can you share how the different components map to my background? I want to understand where the emphasis is."` — 迂回，不直接 push back，收集更多信息再定策略

### B3 · "This is at the top of our band for [Level]"
- **意思**：Base cap 是真的
- **应对**：`"Understood — bands are real. Given base is capped, is there flex on the equity or sign-on side? Those often have different approvers."` — 拆分组件

### B4 · "We're being conservative in this cycle" (股价跌 / hiring freeze rumor)
- **意思**：整体紧缩，不是针对你
- **应对**：`"I hear you. Given the environment, would you consider a stronger sign-on to bridge the equity conservatism? Or a written refresh commitment for Year 1?"` — 用 non-recurring cash 弥补 recurring cut

## Category C · Time Pressure（时间战术）

### C1 · "We need an answer by [tomorrow / EOD / this week]"
- **应对**：`"I want to give this the thoughtfulness it deserves. My decision timeline is [reasonable date, e.g., end of next week]. Is there flexibility to align on that?"` — 反向 anchor deadline
- **如果 recruiter 拒**：这是**你判断他 leverage 的关键信号**。他真急 = 他有 KPI 压力 = 你 leverage 增强
- **应对（如果拒后你怕失去）**：`"OK — help me understand why the tight timeline? If it's driven by [team need / start date / quarterly close], let's find a middle. I want to make this work."`

### C2 · "The team is eager to close — can you decide today?"
- **应对**：`"That's flattering — I'm eager too. But this is a significant decision. Let me sleep on it. I'll come back tomorrow morning with a firm answer."`
- **绝不当场答**

### C3 · "If we can't align this week, we may need to move on"
- 见 [`../prompts/counter-simulation.md`](../prompts/counter-simulation.md) Pushback #3

## Category D · Emotional Manipulation（情绪战术）

### D1 · "I really fought for you internally"
- **意思**：让你 feel guilty about pushing more
- **应对**：`"I don't doubt it — thank you. That's exactly why I want to be direct rather than string this out. Given you've already advocated, let me be clear about what I need to move forward: [ask]."` — Acknowledge + persist

### D2 · "I'm being transparent with you — this is really it"
- **意思**：Trust-based anchor
- **应对**：`"I appreciate the transparency. Let me be equally transparent: [market data + competing offer if any]. Given that, is there any avenue I'm missing?"` — Match transparency with your own

### D3 · "I know how much you want this role"（gently exploits your desire）
- **意思**：他闻到你的兴奋
- **应对**：`"I do want this role — and that's exactly why I want to get the numbers right, so we both feel good long-term. What I'm asking for isn't about the role itself; it's about the components being aligned with the level."` — 分开 "role love" 和 "comp align"
- **教训**：**未来面试和 recruiter 对话中，兴奋要控制**。Interested professional > gushing fan

### D4 · "You're making this harder than it needs to be"
- **意思**：施压让你 back off
- **应对**：`"I understand it feels that way. I'm not trying to be difficult — I'm trying to make sure I understand what's possible before I sign. If the answer is 'no more room,' I can absorb that and move forward. But I'd rather ask clearly."` — 承认 tone + persist

## Category E · Reframe / 转移话题

### E1 · "Have you considered non-cash benefits?" (PTO / WFH / training budget)
- **意思**：想用便宜的东西替代 cash
- **应对**：`"Those are nice, but they don't move my decision. What matters is base / equity / sign-on. Can we focus there?"` — 直接 refocus

### E2 · "Long-term growth here is significant — refresh grants add up"
- **意思**：Refresh 是 speculative，不是承诺
- **应对**：`"That's helpful — and if you'd be willing to put a written refresh commitment for Year 1 in email, I'd factor that into my thinking. Something like: 'eligible for X% refresh in Y1, subject to performance.' Can that be in writing?"` — Force written = force reality check

### E3 · "This level opens up faster promotion opportunities"
- **意思**：Level down-graded 时的安慰
- **应对**：`"I appreciate that framing. If early promotion is realistic, would you support a 6-month performance review (vs. 12) and a formal promotion target in writing?"` — 让他把话说死

### E4 · "The equity has significant upside — it's underappreciated"
- **意思**：Grant 是 low，让你 imagine 未来
- **应对**：`"I'm bullish on the company, which is exactly why I want more equity. If upside is real, adding shares is high-value low-cost for you and high-value for me. Can we increase the share count by 15-20%?"` — 用他的 argument 反 argue

## Category F · Threat / 威胁（低概率但会碰到）

### F1 · "We may need to reconsider the candidate pool"
- 见 [`../prompts/counter-simulation.md`](../prompts/counter-simulation.md) Pushback #3

### F2 · "If you don't accept by X, the offer will be withdrawn"
- **应对**：`"I hear you — I want to be sure I understand. Are you saying that if I don't respond by [time], the offer is rescinded? I want to confirm so I know what's on the table."` — 让他 repeat = 90% back off
- **如果他重复**：**这是真威胁**。你必须马上做 sign / walk 决定，不能 middle ground

### F3 · "Rescinding is up to leadership, but I want to be transparent about the risk"
- **意思**：Deniable threat
- **应对**：`"Understood. Given that risk, let's be efficient. What number would remove the rescind risk entirely? If it's within a reasonable range of my ask, we can close this today."` — 强迫他报 minimum acceptable number

## Category G · Non-Cash（补偿性）

### G1 · Refresh commitment
- Recruiter 主动提 → 你要 written
- 你主动提 → "Would you be open to a written refresh commitment for Year 1, targeting [30% of initial]?"

### G2 · Early performance review
- "6-month vs 12-month review" — recruiter discretion，通常给
- 语言：`"Would you support a formal 6-month performance review, with promotion consideration if metrics are met?"`

### G3 · Start-date bonus / spot bonus
- 少数公司有 "reference bonus"（推荐奖）或 "start bonus"（第一周入职奖）
- 试探性问：`"Are there any spot bonuses or start incentives I should know about?"`

### G4 · Relocation actual cost reimbursement (vs lump sum)
- Lump sum 通常低估
- 语言：`"Can we do actual-cost reimbursement up to $X instead of lump sum? Actual costs typically exceed."`

### G5 · Additional vacation / sabbatical eligibility
- 一般改不动，但可以问
- 弱 leverage 场景可用

## 一句话总结：应对 20 种话术的元规则

1. **不接受 recruiter 的 frame** — 拆解、reframe、pivot
2. **数字用 range，不用单点**
3. **每个 ask 挂 justification**（"given [X], I need [Y]"）
4. **口头承诺一律要 written confirmation**
5. **威胁性话术 name it**（"I don't want to negotiate against a threat"）
6. **24-hour rule** 无差别使用
7. **preserve relationship** — recruiter 是长期 asset
