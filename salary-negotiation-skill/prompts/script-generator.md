# Script Generator

**目标：** 给用户三份**一字不改能用**的脚本 — 📞 电话开场 · 📧 邮件版本 · 💬 recruiter 首次回话模拟（3 种口径）。

**铁律**
- **一字不改能念出来** 是标准。禁止 "you might say something like…" 这种半成品。
- **中英双语**。国内公司 → 中文主稿；美国公司 → 英文主稿 + 中文对照（用户读中文对照理解重点）。
- **不 overclaim**。所有"竞争 offer"、"当前包"数字必须匹配用户 Step 1 提供的真实数据。
- **保留 recruiter 的面子**。任何脚本里都不能让 recruiter 觉得被质疑或愚弄。他是你未来 3-5 年的 comp advocate。

---

## 三份脚本，一次给全

### 📞 Script 1 · 电话开场（首次 counter）

**结构：4 段，每段 1-3 句，全程 60-90 秒念完。**

**Beat 1 · 开场感谢 + 兴奋**（15 秒）
> "Hi [Recruiter Name], thanks so much for getting on the call. I've had a chance to review the offer in detail, and I want to say first — I'm really excited about the team and the [specific product/mission thing you learned in interviews]. This is a role I can see myself in."

（中文对照）"[Recruiter 名字] 你好，谢谢安排这次电话。offer 我认真看过了，先说一句 — 我对 [具体你在面试中了解到的产品/团队/使命] 真的很兴奋，这份工作是我能想象自己长期做下去的。"

**Beat 2 · Pivot 到 comp**（15 秒 · 关键 pivot）
> "That said, before I can formally accept, I want to have an open conversation about the comp components. There are [one / two] pieces I'd love to see if we can move on."

（中文对照）"话虽如此，正式接受前，我想跟你 open 地聊聊 comp 的几项。有 [一 / 两] 项我想看看能不能动一下。"

**Beat 3 · 明确 ask + justification**（30-45 秒 · 核心）

**Modality A（双开）示范：**
> "Two things specifically. First, on base — based on Levels.fyi data for [Company] IC5 in [Location], the median is around $[X], and the current offer is at $[Y]. I'd love to see base come up to around $[target range: A-B]. Second, on the equity grant — the 4-year value is coming in about $[amount] below what I'm seeing at comparable companies. I'd love to see the RSU value at around $[target range]. Together those adjustments would bring the total package to a level where I can confidently say yes."

（中文对照）"两件事。第一，base — 根据 Levels.fyi 上 [公司] IC5 [地点] 的数据，中位数在 $[X]，当前 offer 是 $[Y]，希望能加到 $[target 区间 A-B]。第二，equity — 4 年 total 比同行同 level 低约 $[数字]，希望 RSU 能到 $[target 区间]。两项一起调，我就能 confidently 签。"

**Modality B（单点）示范：**
> "One thing specifically. On the equity grant, the 4-year value is coming in about $[amount] below what I'm seeing at comparable companies for this level. I'd love to see the RSU value at around $[target range]. The base and sign-on I can work with — equity is really the one component I'd love to explore."

（中文对照）"一件事。equity 4 年 total 比同行同 level 低约 $[数字]，希望能加到 $[target 区间]。Base 和 sign-on 我能接受 — equity 是我想聊的那一项。"

**Modality C（收尾）示范：**
> "One small ask before I sign — is there any flexibility on the signing bonus? I'm walking away from about $[X] in unvested equity at my current company, and a bump on sign-on would help bridge that. Even $[10-25K] would make a real difference."

（中文对照）"签之前有个小请求 — sign-on 有没有空间？我在放弃当前公司大约 $[X] 的未 vested equity，sign-on 加一点能补上这个缺口，哪怕 $[10-25K] 都有帮助。"

**Beat 4 · 收尾 · 交球** — 关键：**问完就闭嘴**。别继续解释。
> "I know these need to go through internal approvals, so no pressure to answer right now. Take some time, and I'm around whenever you have an update. Really appreciate you working through this with me."

（中文对照）"我知道要走内部 approval，现在不用马上答。你花点时间，有更新告诉我就行。谢谢你陪我 work through 这些。"

**⚠️ Beat 4 之后立刻停。让 recruiter 说下句。** 沉默是杠杆。用户经常在电话上因为紧张继续 "and, and, and… I mean, it's not a dealbreaker if…" — 这一句 undo 掉整段谈判。**明确告诉用户：说完 Beat 4，喝口水，等他说话。**

### 📧 Script 2 · 邮件版本

**用途**：
- 电话不方便 / 招聘官只用邮件沟通 → 邮件版本作为首触点
- 电话完 1 小时内 → 邮件 confirm 落笔

**结构：4 段，300 字以内。**

**Subject（关键）**：
- 首次 counter：`Following up on offer discussion — [Your Name]`
- 电话后 confirm：`Recap of our call — [Your Name]`
- **绝不写** `Counter Offer` / `Negotiation` / `Reject offer` — 太刺激，让 recruiter 心理防御拉起来。

**Body 模板**（Modality A · 首次 counter）：

```
Hi [Recruiter Name],

Thank you again for the offer for the [Role Title] position on [Team]. After going through it in detail, I want to be direct: I'm very excited about the team and the [specific product / mission thing], and I'd like to find a way to make this work.

There are two components I'd love to discuss:

1. Base — Based on Levels.fyi data for [Company] [Level] in [Location], the P50 is around $[X]. I'd love to see base adjusted to around $[target range: A–B].

2. Equity — The 4-year value is coming in approximately $[amount] below what I'm seeing at comparable roles. A grant adjustment to around $[target range] would bring total comp to a level I can confidently commit to.

I understand these adjustments require internal approval, so there's no rush on a response. I'm happy to hop on a quick call if that's easier than email. Looking forward to next steps.

Best,
[Your Name]
```

（中文对照 · 给国内公司用）

```
[Recruiter 名字] 你好，

再次感谢 [Role Title] 岗位的 offer。仔细看过之后，我想直接说：我对团队和 [具体产品/使命] 非常兴奋，希望能一起把这件事 push 成。

有两项我想聊一下：

1. Base — 参考 [参考数据源] 上 [公司] [Level] [地点] 的数据，P50 约 $[X]。希望能调整到 $[target 区间 A-B]。

2. Equity — 4 年 total 相比同岗同 level 低约 $[数字]。RSU 调整到 $[target 区间] 的话，我能 confidently commit。

我理解这两项调整都需要内部 approval，不急。也可以电话聊，看哪个方式对你更方便。期待下一步。

Best,
[你的名字]
```

**关键 tone 原则**：
- **"I'd love to see" / "希望能"** > "I need" / "I require"（避免最后通牒感）
- **"There's no rush on a response"** — 反直觉但重要。你显得从容，招聘官会更 seriously 对待。
- **"I'd like to find a way to make this work"** — 传递合作意图，不是对抗。
- **绝不说** "if you can't meet this, I'll have to decline" —— 除非你真的 100% 会 walk。

### 💬 Script 3 · Recruiter 首次回话模拟（3 种口径 · 每种都给 1 句应对）

Recruiter 收到你的 counter 之后，头 24-72 小时内会回你**下面 3 种之一**。每种给用户一句 immediate response。（多轮 pushback 见 [`counter-simulation.md`](counter-simulation.md)）

---

**口径 A · "让我去 check 一下再回你"（最常见 · 好信号）**

Recruiter 说：
> "Thanks for the note. Let me take this to the team / internal partners and get back to you by [date]."

→ 你的一句应对：
> "Sounds great, thank you. I'll wait for your update. If it helps, my next major decision point is [Aug 15] — let me know if you need any color from me in the meantime."

**为什么这句**：确认时间线（软性 anchor deadline），不催、不 pull back、不"其实我也可以少一点"。

---

**口径 B · "This is really the best we can do"（挡回 · 中性信号）**

Recruiter 说：
> "I really appreciate the note, but I want to be upfront — this is really our best offer for this level. We built it based on our current bands."

→ 你的一句应对：
> "I understand there are bands to work within, and I appreciate you being direct. Can I ask — if base is fixed, is there any flexibility on the equity side, or perhaps a signing bonus adjustment to bridge the gap? Those two levers might get us closer without touching band."

**为什么这句**：接受 base 是"死的"这个说法，但明确 pivot 到 equity 和 sign-on（这两项通常 approver 不同、预算池不同）。**不 accept "this is our best offer" 作为整体结论**，只 accept 它作为 base 那一项的结论。

---

**口径 C · "让我 loop in 我 manager / 我们可能需要 reconsider 候选人 pool"（弱威胁 · 需坚定回答）**

Recruiter 说（罕见但会碰到）：
> "I'll need to loop in leadership. Just to be transparent — at this level, we'll need to reconsider whether this is the right fit if we can't align quickly."

→ 你的一句应对：
> "That's fair. I want to be equally transparent — I'm genuinely excited about this role, and I'm not trying to be difficult. My counter reflects where the market data is landing for this level. If it turns out this isn't the right budget conversation, I understand, but I'd rather have that conversation than assume. Let me know what leadership says."

**为什么这句**：
- 承认对方 concern（"That's fair"）— 不激化
- 重申兴趣（"genuinely excited"）— 招聘官需要拿这句去 leadership 说 "he still wants it"
- 强化 justification（"market data"）— 让对方拿去卖 approver
- **主动 name 那个威胁**（"If this isn't the right budget conversation, I understand"）— 反向掌控节奏，让 recruiter 知道你不 desperate

**⚠️ 但如果用户 leverage 是 LOW（Modality C / D）** — 这时候被威胁到"reconsider pool" 说明你 push 过头了。此时应对改为：
> "I understand — that's helpful context. Let me be clear: my #1 goal is to join this team. If the numbers I asked for aren't in the cards, I'd rather have that conversation now so we can move forward. Even a smaller adjustment on sign-on would be appreciated, but I don't want to hold up next steps."

**这句话主动 pull back，救 offer**。

## Step 4 · 输出与自检

给用户三份脚本时**必须明确标注**：
- 哪份是电话首触点 vs 邮件首触点（**选一份主用**，另一份 backup）
- 电话打完 1 小时内必须发的 confirm email 模板
- 用户念之前必须**通读一遍**，感受到"哪句自己念不出来"，让 Skill 改成他自己的语气（保留结构，换词句）

**自检 checklist**：
- [ ] 每个数字来自 Diagnosis Step 1 的真实数据，没有虚构
- [ ] Justification 挂了 1-2 条，不是光要数字
- [ ] Beat 4（问完闭嘴）明确告知用户
- [ ] 3 种 recruiter 回答的应对句都给了
- [ ] Modality 匹配 leverage map 的 verdict（HIGH leverage 不给 Modality C 脚本；LOW leverage 不给 Modality A 脚本）
