# Counter Offer Simulation

**目标：** 模拟真实招聘官 3 种硬 pushback，每种走 **至少 2 轮 back-and-forth**，给用户一份"被推回来我该怎么办"的 playbook。

**为什么必须模拟：** 用户第一轮 counter 用了 [`script-generator.md`](script-generator.md) 的脚本，感觉良好。但 recruiter 回来一句 "That's our best" — 用户瞬间 blank。多数负面结局发生在**第二轮**，不是第一轮。

**铁律**
- 每种硬 pushback 必须走 2 轮 back-and-forth（recruiter → user → recruiter → user）。
- 每轮 user 应对必须一字不改可念。
- 每种 pushback 结尾必须写 "**如果他这么说，说明真的没空间了 — 停**" 的 stop-signal。
- 全程扮演真实招聘官，**不要美化**。真实招聘官会施加时间压力、含糊承诺、call your bluff。

---

## Pushback #1 · "This is our best offer / 我们已经给到最好了"

### 招聘官剧本（真实）

**Round 1**（回你首次 counter 之后，通常 48-72 小时）：

Recruiter 语气：温和但坚定，可能加一句"trust me I fought for you"
> "Hi [Name], I really appreciate the thought you put into this. I did take it back to the team, and I want to be transparent — this really is our strongest offer for this level. We built it with your background in mind, and the numbers reflect what we can do. I know it's not what you were hoping for, but I wanted to give you an honest answer rather than string you along."

### 用户 Round 1 应对（3 种，按 leverage 选）

**HIGH leverage 应对**（你有书面 competing offer）：
> "Thanks for going to bat — I do believe you. Let me ask one thing to help me think this through: when you say 'strongest offer,' is that specifically the base, or across all four components? Because I'd like to know where the truly fixed lines are vs. where there's still discretion. For example, base bands and equity approvers are usually different people, so if base is genuinely capped, I want to focus my conversation on the components with actual room."

**为什么这句**：不 accept "strongest offer" 作为整体判定，把它拆成 4 个组件强迫 recruiter 承认哪块真的死了、哪块还有空间。**Recruiter 从没想过被这么 pushback**，通常会松口 "well, sign-on maybe has some flex…"

**MEDIUM leverage 应对**：
> "Appreciate you being direct. Can I ask — if base is truly at the top of what you can do, is there flexibility on either the equity or a signing bonus adjustment? Those tend to sit in different budgets. Even a partial adjustment would help me get comfortable."

**LOW leverage 应对**（无 competing offer，谈判信用有限）：
> "Thank you for being upfront — that helps me. Given that, is there any small adjustment on sign-on possible? If not, I understand, and I'm ready to move forward. I don't want to hold this up."

**注意 LOW leverage 应对：主动**「pull back」**，救 offer**。你已经知道 no room，再 push 一次就变敌人了。

### 招聘官 Round 2（大概率会来一句）

**如果是 HIGH/MED leverage 且你成功拆了组件**：
> "That's fair. Base is genuinely at the top of the band — I can't move that. But let me see if there's some room on the equity side or a sign-on adjustment. Give me a couple of days."

→ **恭喜，你打开了新的谈判 window。** 你的下一句：
> "That would be great. I'm around when you have an update. Thanks again."

**闭嘴，等 recruiter 回。**

**如果 recruiter 说** "Even on those, we're really at our limits":
> "Understood. If you can find even a modest adjustment — say $[小数字，例如 sign-on +$15K] — I think we'd be in a good place. If not, I want to be honest with you so we can move to next steps either way."

**这句是 stop-signal 前的最后一句**。给一个 concrete floor，让 recruiter 明确知道你的 walk-away 线。

### Stop-signal

**如果 recruiter 说** "I've truly exhausted every avenue" **或** "leadership has finalized this as our final position" — **真的没空间了**。走两条路：

1. **接受**：说 "Thank you for pushing on this — I understand. I'm ready to move forward with the offer as-is." 然后**礼貌收尾**。
2. **走**：说 "I really appreciate the effort. Given where the numbers land vs. what I need, I don't think I can move forward with this one. I hope we stay in touch." **明确 walk，不含糊。** 别拖。

**别做**：继续 push 第 3 轮。会 rescind。

## Pushback #2 · "Budget is fixed / 预算固定 / 走不了流程"

### 招聘官剧本

**Round 1**：

Recruiter 语气：官僚，把责任推给"系统"
> "I hear you — unfortunately, our comp bands are set by comp partners at the org level. This offer is at the top of the band already, and there's no mechanism for us to go higher. It's not a personal decision, it's just how our system is structured."

### 用户 Round 1 应对

**拆解式**（HIGH/MED leverage）：
> "That makes sense — bands exist for a reason. Two questions to help me understand: (1) Is the equity grant also band-locked, or is that a separate approval process? (2) Sign-on bonuses are usually outside the base band — is there any flex there? I'm asking because I want to work within your system, not against it."

**为什么这句**：
- **接受"band"这个术语**（不 challenge 制度）
- 强迫 recruiter 澄清 "band" 是不是 cover 4 项（99% 情况：**只 cover base**）
- Sign-on 90% 情况**不在 band 里**（"budget is fixed" 应对不了 sign-on）

**Reframe 式**（如果你有 competing offer）：
> "I get that — bands are real. Can I share something that might help you internally? [Competing Company] is offering $[X] total for this level in the same market. I don't share that to be difficult — I share it in case it helps your comp partners see there might be a market signal worth revisiting. I want to be here, but I also need to make a rational decision."

**为什么这句**：把 competing offer 定位成"帮你说服你的 comp partner"，不是威胁。招聘官可以拿这句原封不动去内部 escalate。

**LOW leverage 应对**：
> "Thanks for explaining. If band is truly fixed, is there any consideration on sign-on or a start-date adjustment that might help bridge? If not, I'm ready to move forward."

### 招聘官 Round 2

**常见回法**：
> "You're right that sign-on and equity have different approvers. Let me check with them and see. I can't promise anything, but I'll come back with what I can find."

→ 你的应对：
> "Really appreciate you exploring this. I'll wait for your update. No rush."

**如果 recruiter 说** "Even those are locked":
> "Understood. Given that, could you help me understand the refresh policy? If year 2 has a meaningful refresh grant, that changes my long-term calculus. Also, is there any consideration for an accelerated first performance review at 6 months instead of 12? Those two things would help me commit."

**为什么问 refresh / early review**：非现金 leverage。招聘官通常有 discretion 承诺"我会 track 你半年 review"，这在 offer letter 里落不了字，但可以在邮件 confirm 里写。**Refresh promise 是招聘官最爱给的 "non-cash" concession**。

### Stop-signal

**"Budget is fixed, refresh is standard, no early review possible"** → 真的没了。同上（Pushback #1）：**接受 or 走**。别第 3 轮。

## Pushback #3 · "We may need to reconsider the candidate pool / 会考虑其他候选人"（弱威胁）

### 招聘官剧本

**Round 1**：

Recruiter 语气：稍微冷了，可能带一点 warning tone
> "I want to be transparent — if we can't align on numbers, we may need to revisit our candidate pool. We have other strong candidates in the pipeline, and while we prefer you, we do need to move forward. So I'd encourage you to think carefully about what number would actually get you to yes."

### ⚠️ 先诊断：这句话是真的还是 bluff？

**招聘官 90% 情况在 bluff**。信号：
- 如果他真的有 2 号候选人可以马上 offer，他不会跟你继续谈 —— 他会直接 rescind + go to 2 号
- 如果他继续跟你磨，说明他还想 close 你（沉没成本 + 找替代人 6 周成本）
- **越是威胁，越说明他没 backup**（真有 backup 的 recruiter 会 quietly walk）

**10% 情况是真的**（信号）：
- 招聘周期很紧（如 Q3 close-out）
- 你之前多次 push 无 justification
- 岗位比较 generic，替代成本低
- Recruiter 语气从 warm 变 cold 的时机（是不是刚 loop in leadership 之后？）

### 用户 Round 1 应对

**Confident 应对**（默认，用于 HIGH/MED leverage）：
> "I appreciate you being direct — I'll be direct too. I'm not trying to squeeze you; my counter reflects genuine market data and where I need to land to say yes with conviction. If it turns out this isn't the right budget for the level, I understand — I'd rather have that clarity now than after signing. Take it back to leadership one more time. If the answer is no, I'll respect that decision, and we can wrap up. But I don't want to negotiate against a threat — I want to negotiate based on what the team can actually do."

**为什么这句（重要）**：
- **Name the threat**（"negotiate against a threat"）— 把它拿到桌面上，让 recruiter 意识到"哦这一招不 work 于这个人"
- **同时给他 face-saving off-ramp**（"if the answer is no, I'll respect that"）— 让他能带着 dignity 回去汇报
- **重申 justification**（"market data"）— 让他有话回 leadership

**LOW leverage 应对**（如果你诊断你没 leverage，别硬扛）：
> "Understood — I hear you. Let me be clear about my #1 priority: I want to be on this team. What number, in your view, would keep this on track? If it's close to where I asked, we're in business. If it's meaningfully lower, I can meet you closer to your side. But I'm asking because I want to close this, not lose it."

**为什么这句**：主动 reset，把决策权还给 recruiter。"让 recruiter 报数字" 是**弱 leverage 时最好的策略** —— 因为你不知道你在哪个 band、不知道对方 walk-away 线。让他 anchor，你可以 slightly 调。

### 招聘官 Round 2

**Confident 应对之后的常见回法**：
> "Fair enough — I hear you. Let me take one more shot at internal escalation. I'll come back by [date]."

→ 你的应对（一句）：
> "Thanks. I'll wait."

**沉默是杠杆。** 别 elaborate。

**LOW leverage 应对之后的常见回法**：
> "I think we can do [some middle number]. Would that work?"

→ 你的应对：
> "Let me think it through overnight — I want to give you a real answer. I'll come back tomorrow."

**永远不当场答 yes**，哪怕数字 OK。**24 小时法则**：让 recruiter 感受到你在认真思考，也让你自己有时间检查数字对不对。

### Stop-signal

**如果 recruiter 说** "Given where we are, we're going to move forward with other candidates. We'll circle back if things change." — **他真 walk 了**。你的应对：

> "I appreciate you letting me know. This role and team are still a place I'd love to be. If circumstances change, please keep me in mind. Wishing you and the team the best."

**别道歉。别哀求。** 保持 relationship。90% 情况下，如果他 2 号 candidate reject 了或者 accept 后闪辞，他会回来找你。**"Circle back" 是招聘官的默认说法**，但**有时是真的**。

**⚠️ 高风险：如果你没 competing offer 且 walk 掉了，你就是待业。诊断时如果 Modality = C/D，永远别让谈判走到 Pushback #3 的 Round 2。**

## Cross-cutting 建议：如何 detect Recruiter 在 bluff vs 说真话

| Recruiter 语言信号 | 大概率是 |
|---|---|
| "This is our best offer" (Round 1) | Bluff — 90% recruiter Round 1 都说这句，作为起手式 |
| "This is our final offer" (Round 2 之后) | 变严重 — 50/50 |
| "We built this with your background in mind" | 温和 anchor，不是 threat |
| "We may need to reconsider the pool" | Bluff 80% 情况；如果连续 2 轮都说，可能是真的 |
| "Let me take this back and get you an answer by [具体日期]" | 好信号 — 他在争取空间 |
| "I need an answer by [紧迫日期]" | Recruiter 在制造 pressure —— 你的 leverage 部分包括"你能不能延" |
| "Leadership has finalized the offer" | 高概率真的 — 但可以试探 "even the equity component?"  |
| 语气变冷 / 邮件回复变慢 | 警告信号 — 可能真的 walking away 或已 loop 了 backup |
| 主动提议 mid-way 数字 | 真的想 close 你 — 通常 accept 是对的（但仍然过夜再答） |

## Step 4 · 输出到 HTML

3 个 pushback × 每个 2 轮 back-and-forth = HTML §5 的 6 个 dialogue rows。每 row：

```
Recruiter (Round X):
  "[原话]"
You (a beat):
  [应对策略解释一句]
You say:
  "[一字不改的应对]"
Then...:
  [下一轮 recruiter 会怎么样]
```

**结尾必须有 3 个 stop-signals**（每个 pushback 一个），明确写清"看到 X 就停止"。
