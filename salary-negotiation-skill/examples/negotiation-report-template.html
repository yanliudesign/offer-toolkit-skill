🚨 Emergency Protocol

IF recruiter says "we need an answer in the next hour": Say: "I need to review with my family / sleep on it — I'll respond by [tomorrow morning]." Do NOT answer on the spot. 说："我需要跟家人商量 / 睡一觉 —— [明天早上] 我给你答复。" 别当场答。
IF recruiter says "if you don't accept, we're pulling the offer": Say: "I hear you. Given that timeline, let me confirm — are you saying the offer will be withdrawn if I don't accept by [time]? I want to be sure I understand." Force him to repeat. 90% will back off. 10% is real → make a firm sign/walk call. 说："我听到了。就这个 timeline 我想确认一下 — 如果我 [时间] 前不 accept，offer 会被撤回，对吗？我想确认理解正确。" 让他重复一次。90% 会 back off。10% 是真的 → 做 sign/walk 决定。
IF you regret rejecting and want to revisit: Call (not email) immediately: "I've reflected on our conversation and would like to revisit if the door is still open." 40% chance door is still open. Do not wait past 48 hours. 立刻打电话（不要邮件）："我重新思考了我们的对话，如果门还开着，我想重新聊。" 40% 情况门还开着。别拖过 48 小时。
IF the revised offer is quietly LOWER on some component vs original: Say: "The updated offer is actually lower than the original on [component]. Was that intentional?" — Force him to answer. 80% is "internal error" and gets rolled back. 说："修改版 offer 在 [组件] 上比原版低了。是有意的吗？" — 强迫他回答。80% 是"内部改错了"，会回滚。
